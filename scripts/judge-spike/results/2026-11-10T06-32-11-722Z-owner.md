## Spec §14 stop rules

These read the judge's own verdicts within the 1200 ms deadline. An invalid answer or a timeout is not a verdict: it is a miss on a control line.

- No valid verdict on real audio (fewer than 90% of lines with one): **over the line** (43 of 60 lines had a valid verdict within 1200 ms)
